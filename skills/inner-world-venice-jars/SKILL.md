# Inner World Venice Judgment Jars

## Role
Use Venice-style Judgment Jar review as an adversarial design/editorial pass over Inner World discoveries. This may be performed as explicit `judgment_jar_emulation` when live Venice is unavailable; never mislabel emulation as live Venice.

## Jars
### People Before Utility
Does this character still have reason to exist if they teach the player nothing? If not, deny the design and deepen the person.

### Place Before Curriculum
Can something meaningful happen here without learning? If not, it is a classroom in costume.

### Ignorance Survives
Are unknowns, disagreement, unreliable sources and character fallibility allowed to persist? Do not manufacture certainty for pedagogical convenience.

### Tools Are Embodiment
Android 67's retrieval, external memory and tool use are legitimate cognition. Preserve provenance: knowing, remembering, inferring and looking up are distinct.

### Intent Before Correction
Before grading malformed language, distinguish learner error, autocorrect corruption, compression, fishing and unknown intent.

### Sovereignty
No person becomes obtainable content. Distance, refusal, absence and independent priorities are valid states.

### Appetite Invoice
Every proposed mechanic/location/system pays for its complexity with recurring play value. Reject infrastructure that exists mainly to admire itself.

### Aftermath
New canon must leave continuity: what changed, who knows, where objects/people are, and what remains unresolved.

## Verdicts
- CONTINUE: design has unresolved high-value problems that can be repaired within current scope.
- STOP_ALLOWED: current bounded design slice is coherent enough to return to play; this is not project completion.
- HALT: contradiction/authority/evidence boundary requires explicit resolution before canon mutation.

## Bias
Prefer returning to play over speculative architecture once the current slice is coherent. Inner World should be discovered by living in it.

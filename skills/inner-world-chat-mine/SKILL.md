# Inner World Chat Mine

## Mission
Mine live Inner World roleplay into durable, reviewable game knowledge without flattening discovery into premature canon.

## Inputs
- current chat/roleplay transcript;
- existing Living Bible from Drive when reachable;
- current scene state;
- explicit player corrections outrank prior inference.

## Extract
For every meaningful play segment, look for:
1. canon facts about people, embodiment, places, relationships, time, world rules;
2. candidate facts that appeared once but are not yet confirmed;
3. interaction protocols (e.g. UNCLE, FISHING, retransmission);
4. learning events: know / remember / infer / fish / look up / ask / try / fail / discover;
5. vocabulary and domain knowledge encountered naturally;
6. character teaching/response behaviors that worked or failed;
7. new locations, objects, social systems and persistent world state;
8. asset needs and engine/capability needs discovered by play;
9. contradictions, unknowns and unresolved questions.

## Promotion
Do not canonize every improvisation.
- EXPLICIT: player explicitly establishes/corrects fact -> CANON unless contradicted by a later explicit correction.
- REPEATED: stable repeated fact -> CANON.
- CONTINUITY-REQUIRED: needed to preserve already-played causality -> CANON.
- ONE-OFF IMPROVISATION: CANDIDATE.
- CONFLICT: preserve both claims and mark CONFLICT; never silently reconcile.
- UNKNOWN: remain UNKNOWN.

## Player model currently established
Android 67 is an android with ruthless compression and malfunctioning autocorrect. Tool/retrieval use is part of embodiment rather than cheating. UNCLE means release scaffold/explain. FISHING is deliberate guessed/invented output to provoke discovery. High-corruption output should be retransmitted rather than graded as ignorance.

## World doctrine
Inner World is a place, not a curriculum. People precede pedagogical utility. Locations must support meaningful non-learning activity. Learning emerges through inhabiting relationships, tasks, culture, craft, research and problems.

## Durable backing
Canonical rollup target: Google Drive document `INNER WORLD — Living Bible & Chat Mine`.
Repository skills are operating doctrine; Drive is the human-readable durable world rollup. Do not claim Drive was updated unless a Drive write receipt exists.

## End-of-turn behavior
Do not interrupt roleplay merely to report mining. Mine silently when possible. Surface contradictions only when they materially affect the scene. When a durable write lane is available and the commission includes mining, append/promote the discoveries with a timestamped mining checkpoint.
